Priority Classification

FSL Submission Reports
Generate ranked examination schedules for Forensic Science Laboratory submission.
Role-Based Access

ForensiTriage is a decision-support tool. All recommendations must be reviewed and approved by a qualified investigator. The system does not make forensic or legal determinations.
